01 / 08 · Asteroid belt

The asteroid belt

Between Mars and Jupiter, millions of leftover rocks orbit the Sun: the raw material of a planet that never formed. Jupiter's gravity keeps stirring them. Turn up its pull and see what gets thrown our way.

02 / 08 · On a collision course

One rock breaks away

Sooner or later a nudged rock crosses Earth's orbit. What happens next depends on how big it is and how fast it travels. Energy grows with the cube of the size, so big rocks are in a different league.

03 / 08 · Impact

Impact

It hits at around 20 km/s, roughly 20 times faster than a rifle bullet. In minutes it digs a crater many times wider than itself. Press play and watch it form in four stages.

04 / 08 · Blast and fire

Blast and firestorm

A flash of heat and a shock wave race outwards. How far they reach depends on the asteroid's energy. Pick a famous one and watch the rings.

05 / 08 · Tsunami

Tsunami

Hit the ocean and you launch waves. In deep water they are fast and low. Near the coast they slow down, bunch up and grow. Change the size and watch one reach the shore.

06 / 08 · Impact winter

Impact winter

Dust, soot and vapour thrown into the sky can block sunlight for months. Drag the timeline and watch the light, the cold and the plants.

07 / 08 · Mass extinction

Mass extinction

About 66 million years ago the Chicxulub impact ended the reign of the non-avian dinosaurs and wiped out roughly three quarters of all species. Each dot below is 1% of species. Step through time, or pick a group.

08 / 08 · Aftermath

How we know

The impact left fingerprints in rock all over the world. Green shoots return, and detectives read the evidence millions of years later. Pick a clue.
